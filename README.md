# Privacy Policy for GP Quiz Game

**Effective date:** September 30, 2026

## 1. Introduction
GP Quiz Game ("we", "our", or "us") is an educational quiz application designed for kids. We respect the privacy of our users, especially children, and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

## 2. Information Collection and Use
Our app is designed to be safe and privacy-friendly for kids:
- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do NOT collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as name, email, address, or phone number).
- **Local Storage:** Progress and high scores are saved locally on your device (`ScorePrefs`) and are not transmitted to any external servers.

## 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)
We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
- The app operates completely offline without tracking user activity across third-party websites or services.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The app does not contain third-party analytics, tracking tools, or external advertisements aimed at collecting user data.
